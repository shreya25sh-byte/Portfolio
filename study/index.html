Before / after interface study

Participants complete 5 everyday tasks in a mock social app, once in the familiar layout (Before) and once after a redesign (After). The kit records time on task, taps, back-presses and give-ups, then a NASA-TLX workload rating after every task.

  1. Enter a participant ID and choose the order.
  2. Hand the device over. Read each task aloud, then they press Start task.
  3. After both rounds, check Results and export CSV.
Order
“Counterbalance” alternates order by participant number, to control for learning effects.
