# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at product companies looking for a UX Researcher or UX Designer. They scan quickly, often on a laptop between other candidates, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn link. Their job: decide in a minute or two whether Shreya can run real research and turn it into design decisions, then open a case study, email her or download the resume.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Shreya Hirpathak, UX researcher, industrial designer and design engineer (M.S. Engineering Design, Penn State '26; B.Des Industrial Design, MIT Institute of Design, Pune). Success means a hiring manager reads at least one case study and makes contact.

## Positioning

A researcher who also builds: survey and scale development validated with statistics (CFA, IRT), plus hands-on prototyping, CAD and hardware. Award-winning, peer-reviewed research (2nd place Best Paper, ASME IDETC-CIE 2026) rather than portfolio-only process work.

## Operating Context

Static HTML/CSS/JS site, no build step. Pages: home (`index.html`) and six case studies in `work/`. Also published as a claude.ai artifact preview. Recruiters arrive cold and skim; case studies are read in depth by hiring managers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive pieces that must stay: the hero colour-lens game with hidden field notes, the 3D Design Jenga tower, the 20-second Quick Demo popups on work cards.
- Inline `style=""` attributes are blocked in the artifact viewer; style through classes or CSSOM.
- No framework; plain CSS appended to `assets/css/style.css`.

## Brand Commitments

- Identity: a "research zine" (paper, ink, riso pink #ff3fa4, serif display) confirmed by Shreya; keep it, remove generic AI tells around it.
- The ink logo (`assets/img/logo-ink.svg`) and riso pink are binding.
- Voice: plain, first person, a little playful; no marketing language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies: MSES thesis (`work/idetc.html`), Innovation vs habit (`work/habit.html`), Totsecure (`work/totsecure.html`), DFAM lattices (`work/dfam.html`), Calidus (`work/calidus.html`), Godrej (`work/godrej.html`).
- Real figures only: 1,910 students surveyed; 16 of 17 items validated; 2 background factors; 520+ students taught; 93.6% gaming-chair gap; 11.4 dB lattice result; 67% assembly reduction.
- Talks: ASME IDETC-CIE 2025 (Anaheim), CERS 2026 (Penn State), ASME IDETC-CIE 2026 (Houston, 2nd place Best Paper).
- No testimonials exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before decoration: every number and interaction should prove a research skill.
2. Work first: case studies are reachable within one scroll.
3. Show the person, not a template: personal artifacts (photos, notes, real charts) over generic UI.
4. Readable by a busy reader: short copy, clear hierarchy, accessible contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA text contrast (4.5:1 body), body text at least 16px, keyboard-usable interactive pieces, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
