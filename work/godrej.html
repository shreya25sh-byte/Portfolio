01 · Overview

Before designing a gaming chair, find out who actually needs one.

Question

Is there a real market for a Godrej Interio gaming chair in India, who is it for, and at what price?

What I did

Benchmarked 17 chairs from 7 brands, ran a 90-person survey and 10 in-depth interviews, then synthesised 4 personas and 3 design briefs.

Outcome

Found a big ownership gap (93.6% had no gaming chair) that shaped Godrej's target positioning across ₹8,000–₹25,000 price tiers.

Internship work. Detailed visuals available on request.

05 · Impact

Research set the positioning.

The insight that most people game but almost none own a gaming chair shaped Godrej's target positioning.Outcome
Lesson

Measure the gap, not just the interest.

“Do you game?” alone shows interest. Asking about setups and ownership revealed the opportunity.

Lesson

Mix methods.

The survey showed what; the 10 interviews explained why. Personas needed both.

Lesson

Tie insights to decisions.

Mapping personas to price tiers turned research into something a product team could act on.

06 · Reflection

What I'd do differently now.

With what I've since learned from scale development, I'd pilot the survey first, check the sample for bias, and follow up with a concept test of the three briefs with the personas they were written for.
