About

Hi, I'm Shreya.

“Ever since childhood, stories have held me in their grip. My storytelling grew from daydreaming into writing, dance and doodling and eventually into prototypes, designs and research.”

I just graduated with an M.S. in Engineering Design from Penn State ('26). My time at Penn State was focused on research, where I studied how demographic backgrounds can affect behaviour and experiences.

I studied User Experience (UX), Human-Centered Design, Design Research and Data Analysis. I've taught 520+ first-year engineers design process and prototyping and presented at ASME IDETC-CIE twice.

I trained as an industrial designer (B.Des, MIT Institute of Design, Pune) and have done five internships making products, the kind that end up in usability reports. That's why I moved toward research.

Looking for UX Researcher / UX Designer roles where research and making sit at the same table.
